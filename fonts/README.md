## Impact

`Impact.ttf` is the copy the shop owner supplied for the Sports tag, and the shop owner is responsible for the
license. It is Monotype's typeface (Impact is a trademark of Stephenson Blake (Holdings) Ltd.). The file's own
license notice reads:

> NOTIFICATION OF LICENSE AGREEMENT
> This typeface is the property of Monotype Typography and its use by you is covered under the terms of a license
> agreement. You have obtained this typeface software either directly from Monotype or together with software
> distributed by one of Monotype's licensees.
> http://www.monotype.com/html/type/license.html

To take it out, delete `Impact.ttf` and its line in `fonts.json`. The Sports tag then asks the visitor's own computer for
Impact (Chrome and Edge) or takes an upload.
