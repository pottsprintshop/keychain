Back

Uses the artwork from the Artwork panel.

Recessed flush into the underside of the base, in a color that contrasts with it: light on a dark base, dark on a light base. Turn the keychain over like a page and it reads correctly. A plate base (Rectangle, Round, Hexagon, under Layers) leaves room for a bigger back. Light-on-dark is a negative image: iPhone Camera and most current Android scanners read a QR code that way, but a few older apps only read dark-on-light; the plate option adds a light square with a border so it reads the usual way.
